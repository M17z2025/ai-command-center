### 9. Later planning permission can affect an enforcement notice, but only to the extent of inconsistency
Town and Country Planning Act 1990 s.180 provides that a notice ceases to have effect so far as inconsistent with a later planning permission for development carried out before that permission. Therefore the precise legal effect of 22/01114/FUL on the 2021 notice must be established, not guessed.

Source:
https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/part/VII/2026-08-10

### 10. Ownership after an unsatisfied compliance period is a serious buyer issue
Current TCPA 1990 s.179 states that after the compliance period, where a required step has not been taken, the person who is then owner is in breach of the notice and may commit an offence, subject to the statutory defence.

Source:
https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1990/8/part/VII/2026-08-10

### 11. Planning/enforcement notices are local land charges
HM Land Registry Practice Guide 79 states that planning and enforcement notices are common local land charges and explains that local land charges can affect purchasers.

Source:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/local-land-charges-pg79/local-land-charges

## Sigma Legal analysis

### Central risk
The buyer is not looking at a conventional property with an old, immaterial planning irregularity. The official record proves a material off-plan build, an enforcement notice, a failed retrospective appeal and specific required alterations to the dwelling and outbuildings.

The later 22/01114/FUL permission may have regularised or superseded parts of the enforcement position under s.180, but permission alone does not establish:
- that the works were actually carried out;
- that the current building matches the approved drawings;
- that all conditions were complied with/discharged;
- that Buildings B and C were altered as required or otherwise lawfully retained;
- that Rochford regards case 20/00051/BLDG_B as satisfied/closed;
- that Building Regulations were satisfied.

### Roof / outbuilding issue
Planning evidence establishes historical planning non-compliance involving the roofs/outbuildings:
- the link wall and dual-pitched canopy between house A and building B was required to be removed;
- building B was required to be reduced to max 4m;
- first-floor roof-void stair/access in B was required to be removed;
- building C was required to be reduced to max 4m.

This must be separated from any alleged physical/structural roof defect. The planning records prove planning-control issues; they do not by themselves prove defective structural construction. An independent structural engineer and full Building Control file are required for the construction-defect question.

## Pre-offer evidence threshold

Sigma Legal advises that the buyer should not submit an unconditional or clean offer as if this were a fully verified compliant property. Before an offer is treated as capable of proceeding, request at minimum:

1. Signed final decision notice for 22/01114/FUL.
2. Full council-stamped approved drawing set for 22/01114/FUL: 100a, 101, 102, 250a-259, and any approved amendments.
3. Signed final decision notice and approved drawings for 22/00868/FUL.
4. Every discharge-of-condition approval and written approval relevant to those permissions.
5. Written Rochford Planning Enforcement confirmation stating:
   - current status of 20/00051/BLDG_B;
   - whether it is satisfied/closed/cancelled/partly superseded;
   - the effect of 22/01114/FUL and 22/00868/FUL on the notice;
   - whether any steps remain outstanding;
   - whether the council has inspected the current house and outbuildings and accepts them as compliant with the operative permissions.
6. Any High Court consent/quashing order and related correspondence concerning the incorrectly issued 22/00867/FUL decision.
7. Independent current measured as-built survey, with overlay/schedule comparing the existing house to 22/01114/FUL and 22/00868/FUL.
8. Separate dimensional/as-built verification for outbuildings B and C and the former link/canopy.
9. Complete Building Control file: application, approved plans, inspections, structural calculations, completion/regularisation certificates for house and all material outbuildings.
10. Independent structural engineer report on the outbuilding roofs if there is any observed/suspected construction defect.
11. Official Land Registry register and plan for EX632083 plus official Local Land Charges search and CON29.
12. Evidence of the seller's legal authority to sell, including any receiver/lender authority or consent required, plus conveyancer-confirmed mechanics for discharge of charges/restrictions at completion.

## Exchange gate

Even if an offer is made subject to due diligence, Sigma Legal advises against exchange until:
- the current physical building has been independently reconciled to the operative permissions;
- Rochford's enforcement position is confirmed in writing;
- Building Control evidence is satisfactory;
- title, local charges, seller authority and secured interests are cleared;
- the purchaser's regulated conveyancer gives a written report on title and confirms authority to exchange.

Do not rely solely on:
- estate-agent particulars;
- a seller statement that the property is compliant;
- the existence of 22/01114/FUL;
- indemnity insurance in place of investigating a known enforcement issue without specific solicitor advice;
- historical planning drawings without a current measured as-built comparison.
