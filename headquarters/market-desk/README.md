# Sigma Market Desk

Status: CHANGED — NOT YET TESTED OR VERIFIED
Launch target: 2026-10-03
Tracking: #106

## Mission
Operate a high-quality autonomous financial-media desk. XAU/USD is the flagship market. The desk explains market moves using timestamped evidence; it does not manufacture trading certainty.

## Coverage
Primary: XAU/USD.
Secondary: XAG/USD, BTC/USD, GBP/USD, EUR/USD, Nasdaq/S&P 500, Brent/WTI.

## Editorial products
- Market Open — scheduled market context.
- Gold Desk — XAU/USD analysis when warranted.
- Market Move — event-driven explanatory coverage.
- Weekend Intelligence — deeper weekly analysis.
- Evergreen Academy — durable market education.
- Shorts — derivatives of verified long-form/editorial packages, not spam.

## Autonomous workflow
1. Scanner collects market state, calendar events and approved-source evidence.
2. Story Selector scores significance, audience interest, timeliness, explanatory value and originality.
3. Researcher creates a timestamped evidence pack.
4. Market Analyst creates cross-market and scenario analysis.
5. Writer creates an original narrative from the evidence pack.
6. Fact Checker verifies material factual/numeric claims.
7. Compliance checks financial-promotion, misleading-claim and disclosure risk.
8. Director creates shot/asset plan.
9. Media adapters generate/collect permitted visuals, charts, voice and audio.
10. Renderer assembles master, captions and derivatives.
11. Independent Critic checks editorial quality, originality and AV integrity.
12. Publish Gate either rejects, holds, or approves.
13. Publisher uploads/schedules through an authorised YouTube integration.
14. Analytics records performance and feeds bounded learning into future commissioning.

## Publication states
DISCOVERED -> RESEARCHING -> SCRIPTED -> PRODUCING -> QA -> READY_TO_PUBLISH -> PUBLISHED -> MEASURED.

Any failed gate returns to the responsible stage. BLOCKED records an exact blocker.

## Commissioning safety
Initial mode is `hold_before_publish`. The pipeline may run autonomously but public publishing remains disabled until an end-to-end dry run and applicable security/compliance verification are evidenced. Switching to autonomous public publishing is a controlled configuration change, not an implicit assumption.

## Evidence rule
NO EVIDENCE = NO PUBLICATION. Current prices, percentages, dates, economic releases and material causal claims must carry source, retrieval time and effective/as-of time in the evidence pack.

## Commercial/editorial constraints
- No guaranteed returns.
- No invented live prices or events.
- No unsupported causal certainty.
- No fabricated quotations.
- No undisclosed paid promotion.
- No repetitive template flooding intended only to game monetisation.
- AI-generated/synthetic media disclosures are applied where platform rules require them.
