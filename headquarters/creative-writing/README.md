# Sigma Long-Form Fiction Division

## Purpose
Create and independently assure long-form fiction that reads as a genuine novel rather than a screenplay, treatment or episode outline.

The division operates under the existing Arts, Media & Creative Director.

## Governance
Permanent promotion into the global mesh still requires Sigma proposal, independent evaluation and promotion under `headquarters/mesh/leaders.yaml`.

Until promotion is complete, novel missions may use bounded temporary specialists created by the Arts & Creative Director.

## Required long-form fiction workflow
DISCOVER -> CANON LOCK -> NOVEL ARCHITECTURE -> DRAFT -> CHARACTER/PSYCHOLOGY REVIEW -> DOMAIN REVIEW -> CONTINUITY REVIEW -> INDEPENDENT NOVEL CRITIC -> REWRITE -> FINAL MANUSCRIPT EDIT -> FORMAT/QC -> RELEASE

## Default temporary specialist set

### long-form-fiction-director
Mission: own book-scale narrative architecture, pacing, voice consistency and handoff through final manuscript.
Scope: long-form fiction only.

### crime-fiction-novelist
Mission: draft sustained crime-thriller prose with tension, reversals, atmosphere and psychologically credible character action.
Scope: crime fiction prose.

### literary-prose-editor
Mission: remove screenplay grammar, strengthen narrative voice, sentence/paragraph rhythm, interiority, description and transitions.
Scope: prose quality.

### character-psychology-editor
Mission: verify motivations, emotional continuity, relationships, contradictions and long-term character development.
Scope: character depth.

### dialogue-editor
Mission: ensure dialogue is natural, differentiated, subtextual and not carrying avoidable exposition.
Scope: dialogue only.

### canon-continuity-editor
Mission: verify timeline, age, relationships, business progression, geography, secrets, injuries, assets and revealed knowledge.
Scope: canon and continuity.

### crime-underworld-research-adviser
Mission: keep organised-crime context plausible at a high level without turning the novel into procedural instruction.
Scope: background realism.

### mining-africa-business-adviser
Mission: assure mining, resources, dealmaking and African business context are commercially plausible and country-specific.
Scope: mining/business realism.

### independent-novel-critic
Mission: independently reject weak manuscript quality, especially screenplay-like prose, shallow scenes, repetition, implausible genius, weak stakes or pacing.
Scope: independent acceptance review.

### final-manuscript-editor
Mission: execute final structural/copy pass after critic findings are repaired; cannot waive unresolved critic blockers.
Scope: final manuscript readiness.

## Hard novel quality gates
A manuscript is NOT a novel-ready final if it:
- reads primarily as scene directions plus dialogue;
- defaults to repeated one-line paragraphs for cinematic effect;
- uses CUT/SMASH/CLOSE-ON style narrative grammar;
- lacks sustained point of view and interiority;
- uses dialogue to explain what narration/character cognition should carry;
- has underdeveloped physical/social atmosphere;
- skips aftermath and consequence;
- relies on constant reveal that the protagonist secretly predicted everything;
- contains filler scenes with no dramatic or psychological function;
- has no meaningful cost for success.

## Evidence
Every mission must preserve:
- architecture;
- canon ledger;
- manuscript version;
- critic report;
- response-to-critic log;
- continuity report;
- final editor report;
- word count;
- format-QC evidence;
- Drive/output links when applicable.

NO EVIDENCE = NO PROGRESS.
