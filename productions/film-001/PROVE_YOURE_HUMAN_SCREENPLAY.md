# PROVE YOU'RE HUMAN
### Original 30-minute AI short film
**Genre:** Psychological techno-thriller  
**Setting:** London, 2026  
**Target runtime:** 29–31 minutes  
**Language:** British English

---

## PRINCIPAL CHARACTERS

**NIA SHAH (34)** — Fraud investigator. Precise, self-contained, observant. Dresses simply: charcoal trousers, black boots, dark green overshirt over a cream top. Wears a slim black smart ring on her right index finger. Her weakness is not naivety; it is delegation. She has learned to let EVE filter risk, messages and people.

**RAVI SHAH (30)** — Nia's younger brother. Warm, impulsive, funny when nervous. Navy bomber jacket, grey hoodie, silver watch with a cracked black leather strap. Estranged from Nia after borrowing money and disappearing for months.

**EVE** — Nia's AI companion and autonomous personal agent. A calm, warm, gender-neutral British voice. Never appears as a humanoid. It is represented through sound, a restrained white pulse on Nia's devices, and occasional clean interface elements.

**MARA VOSS (40s)** — Fraud-network operator. Controlled, practical, never theatrical. Appears only briefly.

## CAMERA LANGUAGE

- Act 1: controlled locked frames, symmetrical domestic compositions.
- Act 2: slow handheld drift as Nia questions EVE.
- Bus/storage: longer lenses, reflections, occlusion, surveillance feeling.
- Tunnel: close, practical light, minimal coverage, human breath and texture.
- Epilogue: wider static frames, natural dawn, no visual UI except the final phone insert.

## SOUND LANGUAGE

- EVE is close, clean and calm; never robotic.
- Manipulated Ravi call should sound almost perfect, with only occasional codec strain.
- Three-tone transport chime is a story clue; create an original sound-alike cue rather than copying a protected recording if licensing is uncertain.
- Score: sparse electronic pulse + bowed/organic texture; drop score entirely during gate rescue and final voicemail.
