# THE LINE — S01E01 "THE DRIVER"
## Shooting Script v1
**Runtime target:** 28–32 minutes
**Story date:** Sunday 27 September 2026 and the preceding 48 hours
**Rating target:** 15

---

## SCENE 1 — EXT. LONDON DOCKLANDS / INDUSTRIAL ROAD — NIGHT
Rain beads across sodium-lit tarmac. A dark electric performance saloon cuts through the docks almost silently apart from tyre hiss, suspension thump and rising inverter whine.

Inside, TJ PATEL drives with total concentration. KAI PATEL, younger, sharper dressed, holds a hard metal case on his lap.

KAI
Please tell me they’re not actually shooting at us.

A round punches through the rear glass.

TJ
They’re shooting.

KAI
That wasn’t the question.

Two premium electric SUVs close from behind.

Atlas is NOT in the car; TJ made sure he was left safely at the garage before this run.

TJ spots a container transporter ahead.

TJ
Seatbelt.

KAI
TJ—

TJ rotates the car through a violent, precise handbrake turn, threads under the trailer’s turning arc, and disappears down a service lane.

CUT TO BLACK.

TITLE: THE LINE

SUPER: 48 HOURS EARLIER

---

## SCENE 2 — INT. PATEL PERFORMANCE — DAY
A modern but modest East London electric-performance garage. Worn floor. High-end diagnostics. Half-finished conversion projects.

TJ works under an EV on a lift.

ATLAS, his black-and-rust Doberman with natural ears and tail, lies nearby.

AMIRA PATEL enters with coffees.

Atlas rises, tail relaxed, and greets her affectionately.

AMIRA
You slept here again.

TJ
I went home.

AMIRA
At what time?

TJ
Technically morning.

KAI enters late.

TJ
You’re late.

KAI
I’m entrepreneurial.

TJ
That usually means expensive.

A customer asks whether the repair will be done today.

TJ promises it will.

After the customer leaves, Kai quietly shows TJ an overdue rent notice.

TJ’s face doesn’t change.

Atlas watches him.

---

## SCENE 3 — EXT. PATEL PERFORMANCE — LATER
A Bentley Torcal S glides to a stop.

Atlas gets to his feet before TJ hears anything.

MARCUS VALE steps out. Immaculate, restrained, modern wealth.

Atlas positions himself between Marcus and TJ but does not bark.

MARCUS
He likes me.

TJ
No. He’s being polite.

Marcus smiles.

He needs a discreet repair on a high-end EV damaged during “private transport”.

TJ refuses anything criminal.

Marcus studies him.

MARCUS
Your father used to say the same thing.

TJ stiffens.

MARCUS
Until he needed money.

TJ
Don’t talk about my father.

Marcus leaves a card.

MARCUS
One day you’ll realise principles cost more than petrol ever did.

He looks at the EV charger.

MARCUS
Or electricity.

---

## SCENE 4 — EXT. COMMUNITY FOOTBALL GROUND — AFTERNOON
TOMMY QUINN coaches a chaotic multicultural youth side.

TOMMY
Football is very simple. Twenty-two people chase a ball and the person who learns when not to chase it usually wins.

A PLAYER raises a hand.

PLAYER
Is that actually football advice?

TOMMY
Mostly relationship advice.

Kai watches from the sideline, distracted by his phone.

A message arrives:
**£20K. ONE COLLECTION. NO DRUGS. NO WEAPONS. EASY.**

Tommy notices.

TOMMY
Bad news?

KAI
Opportunity.

TOMMY
They often wear the same coat.

---

## SCENE 5 — INT. MARCUS VALE PRIVATE DINING ROOM — NIGHT
Marcus hosts politicians, financiers and executives.

His daughter LENA VALE moves easily among them.

A lieutenant leans in.

LIEUTENANT
Blackthorn wants assurance on Friday’s movement.

MARCUS
They always want assurance.

LIEUTENANT
This one’s different.

Marcus notices VICTOR SLOANE across the room.

Victor raises his glass.

Marcus does not return the gesture.

---

## SCENE 6 — INT. NOAH COLE’S HOTEL ROOM — NIGHT
NOAH COLE has built a wall of evidence.

Shipping manifests.
Land maps.
Photos.
Blackthorn logos.
One photograph of MAYA COLE.

He plays Maya’s last voicemail.

MAYA (V.O.)
Noah... if you get this, something went wrong. There’s a company. Blackthorn Logistics. Don’t go to the police. Don’t trust—

The message cuts.

Noah freezes on a photograph of HAWTHORNE ESTATE.

---

## SCENE 7 — EXT. HAWTHORNE ESTATE — MORNING
ELLIE HAWTHORNE rides across the estate in an electric utility 4x4.

Her brother DANIEL waits with paperwork.

DANIEL
Thirty-eight million.

ELLIE
Still no.

DANIEL
We owe eight.

ELLIE
Then we’re worth thirty.

DANIEL
That is not how debt works.

ELLIE
It is how stubbornness works.

Daniel tells her Blackthorn has increased its offer.

ELLIE
Why does a logistics company want farmland?

Daniel avoids her eyes.

---

## SCENE 8 — INT. PATEL FAMILY HOME — EVENING
Amira cooks. TJ fixes something at the kitchen table. Atlas rests with his head on TJ’s foot.

Local radio plays quietly.

RADIO HOST (V.O.)
...a mild end to September, but forecasters say a colder Atlantic system could move in next week...

Kai arrives.

TJ notices he’s dressed to go out.

TJ
Where?

KAI
Work.

TJ
What work?

KAI
The kind that pays.

Amira gives Kai a look.

AMIRA
That sentence has never ended well.

---

## SCENE 9 — EXT. EAST LONDON CAR PARK — NIGHT
Kai meets a contact.

He is given access to an electric executive car and told to drive it from Felixstowe to London.

No drugs.
No passengers.
No weapons.

Just the car.

KAI
Twenty thousand for a car?

CONTACT
For not asking questions.

Kai takes the key.

---

## SCENE 10 — INT. BLACKTHORN OPERATIONS FLOOR — NIGHT
A sterile logistics control room.

VICTOR SLOANE watches live route data.

SOFIA AL-HASSAN observes from a separate intelligence feed.

Her superior tells her to stay off Blackthorn.

SOFIA
We’ve spent four years getting close.

DIRECTOR
And now you’re being told to stop.

SOFIA
By who?

No answer.

She understands.

---

## SCENE 11 — EXT. FELIXSTOWE / A12 — NIGHT
Kai collects the car.

At a charging stop, he notices the rear storage compartment does not sit flush.

He checks it.

Inside: the metal case.

He calls TJ.

KAI
Don’t shout.

TJ
That depends on the next sentence.

KAI
I may have found something.

TJ
Then leave it where you found it.

KAI
Slight complication.

Blackthorn security arrives.

Kai runs.

---

## SCENE 12 — INT. PATEL PERFORMANCE — NIGHT
TJ receives Kai’s location.

Amira sees his face.

AMIRA
What did he do?

TJ grabs keys.

Atlas rises.

TJ kneels in front of him.

TJ
Stay.

Atlas stares at him.

TJ
Guard Amira.

Atlas turns and sits beside Amira.

TJ leaves.

---

## SCENE 13 — EXT. DOCKLANDS / INDUSTRIAL ROADS — NIGHT
This becomes the full cold-open chase.

TJ reaches Kai in a customer performance EV.

Kai jumps in with the case.

Security gives chase.

The sequence is EV-specific:
- savage instant acceleration
- tyre scrub
- silent launch
- regen braking under hard deceleration
- heavy vehicle mass
- no fake combustion audio

They escape through Docklands.

---

## SCENE 14 — INT. PATEL PERFORMANCE — LATE NIGHT
TJ and Kai return.

Atlas charges to TJ, checks him, then settles once he sees TJ is unharmed.

Amira arrives furious.

AMIRA
You left me with the dog and drove into a gunfight?

TJ
He’s better company.

They open the case.

Inside is a hardened data drive.

Kai plugs it into an isolated machine.

A file structure appears.

**THE LINE**

Routes.
Companies.
Names.
Government departments.
Freight corridors.

TJ sees one archived signature.

**RAVI PATEL**

His father.

---

## SCENE 15 — INT. MARCUS VALE RESIDENCE — SAME NIGHT
Marcus receives a call.

LIEUTENANT
The package is missing.

MARCUS
Who has it?

LIEUTENANT
Two brothers.

Marcus barely reacts.

LIEUTENANT
TJ Patel and Kai Patel.

Marcus goes still.

MARCUS
Patel?

He crosses to an old locked drawer.

Inside: a faded photograph.

Young MARCUS.
VICTOR SLOANE.
RAVI PATEL.

All three beside a freight truck.

Marcus looks deeply unsettled.

---

## SCENE 16 — INT. PATEL FAMILY HOME — DAWN
TJ calls Amira.

No answer.

He calls again.

Atlas is suddenly alert.

TJ enters the house.

A chair overturned.

A mug shattered.

Amira’s phone on the floor.

Atlas growls low.

TJ looks up.

Pinned to the wall:

A black card.

**RETURN WHAT ISN’T YOURS.**

Kai enters behind him.

KAI
TJ...

TJ picks up the card.

His expression changes.

Not panic.

Decision.

TJ
Find Marcus Vale.

CUT TO BLACK.

END OF EPISODE 1.
