# THE LINE — Shot QC & Anti-AI Gate v1

Every shot receives PASS / REPAIR / REGENERATE.

## Checks
1. face identity locked;
2. eyes/teeth/ears/hands anatomically correct;
3. Atlas markings/ears/tail/collar consistent;
4. wardrobe look and damage state correct;
5. vehicle make/geometry/wheels/interior consistent;
6. reflections/shadows physically plausible;
7. lighting direction matches scene;
8. motion/physics believable;
9. camera movement physically possible;
10. background people not duplicated/warped;
11. signage/text legible or intentionally absent;
12. lip sync and eyelines correct;
13. room tone/sound perspective correct;
14. no plastic skin, over-bloom, fake bokeh, over-sharpened AI texture;
15. continuity with previous and next shot.

## Final audience test
Would a viewer assume this was photographed live-action? If not, it fails.
