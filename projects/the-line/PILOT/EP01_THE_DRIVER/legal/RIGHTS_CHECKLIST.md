# THE LINE — Legal / IP / Rights Checklist v1

## Originality
- Characters, plot, dialogue, production design and score must remain original.
- Genre influences may inform pacing/tone only; do not copy protected characters, distinctive dialogue, scenes, logos or storylines.
- No living-actor likeness unless explicitly licensed.
- No cloned celebrity voices.

## Vehicles / brands
- Real vehicle names may be referenced where lawful, but marketing-style endorsement must not be implied.
- If exact marque/logo rendering creates licensing or generation issues, use fictionalised equivalents while preserving vehicle class/performance logic.
- Avoid defamatory fictional conduct tied to real manufacturers.

## Music
- Original score and original songs only unless a track is licensed.
- Keep provenance for generated music, prompts, tool/provider, plan terms and export date.
- Human-authored lyrics preferred for commercial chain-of-title.

## Voices
- Use created/licensed voices with documented rights.
- No impersonation of identifiable public figures.

## News/radio
- Real-world news must be factually contemporaneous to story date.
- Do not insert real people/companies into fictional crimes or corruption.
- Fictional local news/radio is preferred for plot exposition.

## Animal welfare portrayal
- Atlas depicted with natural ears/tail and responsible handling.
- No gratuitous animal harm.

## Release file
Before distribution, maintain a rights ledger for: script, voices, music, generated assets, logos/brands, stock, fonts, and third-party model licences.
