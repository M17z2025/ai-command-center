# THE LINE — Voice & Dialogue Bible v1

## Global
Natural contemporary UK delivery. No theatrical gangster voices. Overlap, pauses, breaths and unfinished thoughts are allowed. Avoid exposition that characters would not naturally say.

## TJ Patel
East London British-Indian. Calm, low-mid register, economical phrasing. Rarely raises his voice; anger gets quieter.
Catch rhythm: short answers, dry humour, decisive commands under pressure.

## Kai Patel
East London British-Indian. Faster pace, more slang, emotionally transparent. Uses humour to cover fear.

## Amira Patel
Warm, educated British-Indian. Direct, clear, confident. Family teasing should feel effortless.

## Marcus Vale
Black British, polished London delivery. Measured, elegant, never cartoon-posh. Uses silence as pressure.

## Tommy Quinn
Irish-English. Warm, quick, lightly comic. Advice often sounds like jokes until it lands.

## Noah Cole
Mixed British-American cadence. Sparse speech. Low register. Never macho for the sake of it.

## Sofia Al-Hassan
British-Moroccan. Precise, professional, contemporary London accent. Field voice is concise and neutral.

## Ellie Hawthorne
Essex/SE England educated rural owner. Practical, unsentimental, not aristocratic.

## Victor Sloane
Neutral executive British. Calm corporate language; menace comes from certainty rather than volume.

## Production
Create one locked voice identity per principal character. Maintain speaking rate, pitch range, breathiness, accent, and mic distance across episodes. No celebrity imitation.
