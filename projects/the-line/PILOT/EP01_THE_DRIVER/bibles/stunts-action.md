# THE LINE — Stunt & Action Bible v1

## Rating
Target UK 15. Intense but not gratuitous. Threat and consequence matter more than gore.

## EV chase physics
- instant torque, not engine rev build;
- substantial battery mass;
- strong regen deceleration;
- realistic tyre load and wet grip;
- low centre of gravity;
- avoid endless drifting;
- no fake gear-change choreography unless vehicle genuinely simulates it.

## SC01 / SC13 chase design
1. pursuit begins on broad dock road;
2. Blackthorn SUVs close using mass and road positioning;
3. rear-glass impact escalates stakes;
4. TJ uses route knowledge rather than impossible speed;
5. hard braking/regen creates separation;
6. transporter turn creates narrow crossing window;
7. TJ commits under turning trailer arc;
8. pursuers forced wide;
9. service-lane escape.

## Fighting
Close, fast, ugly, grounded. Use environment and leverage. No superhero durability.

## Atlas
Never used as a disposable attack device. Protective intervention only against immediate threat; believable commands and recovery.

## Safety/AI QC
No impossible limb motion, vehicle deformation, dog motion, firearm handling, or collision physics. Regenerate rather than hide bad geometry.
