# THE LINE — Music & Sound Bible v1

## Score identity
Modern UK crime drama with multicultural texture. Original only.

### TJ motif
UK garage / restrained grime pulse / sub-bass / sparse piano. Tempo 120–138 depending on scene.

### Marcus motif
Modern soul harmony, upright/electric bass, brushed percussion, restrained luxury hip-hop texture.

### Blackthorn motif
Low industrial pulse, metallic percussion, filtered synth pressure.

### Tommy/community motif
Warm guitar, keys, light brass/soul influence; emotionally sincere, never twee.

### Noah/Sofia motif
Minimal electronic rhythm, low strings, dry percussion.

### End-credit song
Working title: EVERYBODY HAS A LINE.
Theme: loyalty, compromise, power, family, crossing boundaries. Original lyrics and composition only.

## EV sound
Tyres, wind, suspension, body resonance, inverter/drivetrain whine, road texture, impact. No fake V8 beds.

## Radio/news
Recurring fictional station: MERCY FM / CITYLINE NEWS (working names). Each episode advances exactly 7 days; weather/news/football chatter update to story week. Real-world references require date verification and no defamatory fictional linkage.
