# THE LINE — Sigma Production Departments

## Script Writers Room
Lock pilot screenplay, cold open, act turns, character voice, cliffhanger, and season continuity.

## Casting
Create unique original appearance specs; prioritize chemistry, recognisability and repeatable AI consistency. No direct resemblance to living actors unless licensed.

## Wardrobe / Costume
2026 UK contemporary. Clothing communicates class trajectory. Jay starts functional/affordable and evolves gradually; Marcus begins immaculate/luxury; Tommy is warm practical sportswear; Sofia has low-profile professional intelligence styling.

## Hair / Makeup
Continuity-first. Maintain fixed hair, facial hair, skin details, scars, injury progression and time-of-day continuity.

## Locations / Production Design
Reusable hero sets: Malik Performance, Malik family home, Vale residence/private venue, football ground, Hawthorne Estate, Blackthorn warehouse, London Docklands/industrial roads, operations/interview interiors.

## Stunts / Action
Design around realistic EV characteristics: instant torque, regenerative braking, low centre of gravity, battery mass. Avoid impossible petrol-engine cues. No gratuitous violence; intensity suitable for 15+.

## Vehicles
Primary action fleet should be modern EVs. Candidate categories: high-performance electric saloon/GT, electric SUV, electric commercial van, modified EV workshop project, premium executive EV. Exact makes/models remain subject to visual/IP/licensing and generation reliability.

## Sound / Music
Create an original sonic identity. EV action needs tyre, suspension, wind, drivetrain whine, road resonance and designed low-frequency tension rather than fake V8 audio.

## Continuity / QC
Every recurring character, vehicle, costume, set and prop receives an immutable asset ID and approved reference pack.
