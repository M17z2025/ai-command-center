# THE LINE — Book One to Screen Adaptation Map

## Source
Book One: THE SHOP

## Recommended adaptation
Two seasons of 8 x ~30 minutes.

### Season 1
Focus: shop, home, cashflow, first commercial fixes, Marcus introduction, first fee.

Finale:
M invoices properly for strategic advice and realises judgement can become a business.

### Season 2
Focus: distribution, second shop, scaling problems, business rescue, equity lesson, home/business boundary, first serious Marcus assignment.

Finale:
M forms his advisory business and receives the first Africa-facing call.

## Pilot concept — NEW
The new pilot should NOT begin with a giant action chase.

Cold open:
M resolving three apparently unrelated problems from behind the counter of his mobile-phone shop while customers constantly interrupt him.

By the end of the opening sequence the audience understands:
this man sees systems.

Then we meet Kay, Asha and Atlas.

The hook is competence and character first; crime/action grows later.

This makes his later transformation into Bossman far more satisfying.

## Action curve
Season 1: low physical action, high commercial/social tension.
Season 2: occasional threat and pressure.
Seasons 3–4: crime-adjacent danger grows.
Seasons 5+: international/mining/security action can become substantial.

Cars and luxury also escalate with M.
Do not give the future Bossman lifestyle to the shopkeeper version.
